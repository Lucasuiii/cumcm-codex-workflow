# v0.4 independent-review and reader-facing paper design

Version 0.4 is the complete, standalone contract for the seven-stage workflow. It is not a compatibility layer for v0.3 projects. Existing projects must be deliberately reconstructed into the v0.4 contracts; missing evidence is never inferred.

## Independent review before validation

`build_independent_review_package.py` creates a conclusion-withheld package containing the official inputs, problem and model contracts, computation entry points, run records, and executed outputs. It also embeds a small reviewer Skill and a structured result template.

The originating task stops after packaging. The user chooses a human or model reviewer and records the separate task reference. A same-conversation review cannot pass. A same-model fresh task is explicitly marked as correlated, while a different model or human may be marked independent. None of these labels turns the review into mathematical proof.

The raw review is preserved verbatim in `INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_RAW.md`; `INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_RESULT.json` records its reviewer identity, scope, findings, and verdict. Major defects return to the earliest affected stage. Missing user-supplied official material produces an inconclusive result rather than authorizing network search.

## Reader-facing paper and internal sidecars

The final paper is written for judges, not for the workflow engine. Internal IDs, evidence-state enums, local paths, run coverage, and gate language remain in `PAPER_TRACEABILITY.json` and other JSON sidecars. `paper_visible_text_check.py` extracts rendered PDF text and blocks known metadata leakage. Excessive decimal precision and number-dense sentences are review flags that require revision or a reader-facing justification.

`PAPER_PLAN.json` adds a reader narrative and keeps the full evidence route outside the visible manuscript. Content review checks mechanism explanation, derivation, result interpretation, numerical presentation, abstract synthesis, conclusion directness, and reference-style transfer. Same-context content review or final QA cannot finalize the paper.

The bundled `cumcm-contest-ctex` scaffold is modular and question-driven. It does not insert a table of contents by default. Each question moves from task interpretation and mechanism through model construction, algorithm, result explanation, validation, and limitations. The scaffold remains submission-neutral until the user supplies the current official rules or template and the compliance review is recorded.

## Delivery boundary

Delivery requires three separately addressable roles:

1. the exact reviewed final PDF;
2. editable LaTeX source with its entry point and required assets;
3. computation source with its entry point and rerun instructions.

The compliance check uses only user-supplied official materials. If a required rule or template is missing, delivery is reported as blocked and the user is asked for that material. Submission itself remains user-controlled.

## Narrow digest policy

SHA-256 is retained only where a byte-for-byte identity question matters:

- official sources whose later alteration would change the problem;
- formal computation inputs and claim-bearing outputs;
- the small set of stage contracts covered by an explicit approval, so post-approval drift is detectable;
- the exact PDF reviewed for paper QA and delivery.

Decision events are append-only but are not hash-chained. Ordinary code, LaTeX, documentation, editing-stage figures, compile logs, caches, temporary files, and support files do not require digests. If an optional digest is present and stale, it is normally a warning. People approve visible artifacts and summaries; they never have to inspect a 64-character digest.

## Evidence boundary

Schema checks, token linting, successful execution, and an accepted review establish workflow evidence only. They do not prove the task interpretation, mathematical formulation, implementation, statistical validity, or global optimality. Strong claims still require claim-specific certificates and executed evidence.
